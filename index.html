FAQ

Good questions. Clear answers.

Do guests need an app?

No. The intended guest journey is browser-first: tap the link, read the details and RSVP.

Can we send it on WhatsApp?

Yes. The invitation and website link can be shared directly through WhatsApp, text or other channels.

Can each guest receive a personal invite?

Yes. Personalised guest links can be offered as an add-on for segmented guest lists.

Can our website include our schedule?

Yes. Add ceremony, reception, photos, dress code, directions, FAQs and other guest information.

Can we use our own photos?

Yes. Your imagery is one of the main ways the experience becomes personal to your story.

Can we change details later?

Website content can be structured so important wedding information can be updated as plans evolve.
